# Data Analyst, Growth — Fictional Streaming Co. (example job post)

We're looking for a Data Analyst to help our Growth team understand what drives subscriber sign-ups and retention.

**You will**
- Design and analyze A/B tests on onboarding and pricing pages
- Build dashboards in Tableau or Looker for product and marketing stakeholders
- Model churn and lifetime value to prioritize retention campaigns
- Write production SQL and maintain data pipelines with dbt

**You have**
- 2+ years of analytics experience
- Strong SQL and Python (pandas)
- Experience with experimentation and statistics
- Clear communication with cross-functional stakeholders
- Nice to have: dbt, Snowflake, causal inference
